# Turning stealth craft into provable systems

The best stealth and sniper games already agree on a toolkit for every lane Sightline's labs cover: nested vision zones feeding a filling meter with asymmetric rise and fall; hearing measured as path distance on a coarse graph rather than in straight lines; guards built as a small state machine with utility scoring inside each state and a coordinator above; gravity plus one drag constant per round, read through mil instruments; bounded collide-and-slide controllers; camps assembled from handmade modules and generated-and-tested under fixed retry budgets; and several anti-camping levers working together. Almost all of it was tuned by playtest, publishes few numbers, and cheats exactly where Sightline promises not to: The Last of Us shortened its searches by checking the player's true position, Splinter Cell: Blacklist halves hearing for guards offscreen, Sniper Elite III reveals the shooter to the whole map on his third shot, and Far Cry lets a sniper shoot the alarms out from range. Sightline's deterministic fixed-step simulation, pure-function routines, per-entity random streams and perception boundary let each lab replace those compromises with properties checked over the whole input space (monotone, symmetric, telegraphed, calibrated) and with quantities computed exactly, such as the shock cone that makes a suppressed rifle's crack point the wrong way, the safe intervals between a patrol's glances, and the changing graph of which guards watch each other. No shipped game documents checks like these, and the stealth research closest to them stops at small floor plans. The most urgent finding is numeric: the design's sight factors span about 800× before distance applies, so scaling the fill rate alone cannot keep a still, prone player in cover nearly invisible beyond 50 m without range caps or a decay that outruns the fill, and no shipped game gives peripheral vision long range. Each lab below gets a starting point, its shortfall, a way past it and a test that shows it got there, while the numbers nobody has published (masking thresholds, localization error, sway amplitudes, carry noise, radio delays) are the labs' to measure.

## Seven lanes converge on proven craft, tuned by feel

The strongest sources are developer chapters and talks (Game AI Pro, GDC), open configuration and source code (The Dark Mod, Brogue, Quake III, Source, ACE3, Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup), datamined tables (Metal Gear Solid V) and a thin academic line centred on McGill. Guides, fan wikis and forum posts fill the gaps on Metal Gear, Far Cry and Ghost Recon escalation, and the text flags them where they carry a claim. Figures marked *computed* were calculated during this research from the cited inputs. The scripts are in `docs/research/scripts/` (`ballistics.py`, `closedform.py`, `zeros.py`, `extra.py`, `incline.py`, `validate.py` and `flood.mjs`).

### Sight nests its zones, gates on body rays and smooths both edges

Shipped stealth games never rely on one cone. Blacklist layers a core cone where detection is fast, a wider peripheral shape and, for distance, a coffin-shaped box that widens and then narrows again, because a plain cone let guards notice far players well off to the side; whichever shape is most inclusive sets the timer's range, and the time within that range scales linearly with distance ([Walsh, Game AI Pro 2](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)). The timer starts only when enough of **eight rays from the guard to the player's bones** are clear, with a minimum per stance, and a lit, unobstructed player inside a shape is caught within a couple of seconds at most ([Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)). Metal Gear Solid V's datamined tables express the same idea as range-and-angle zones switched by situation: a soldier's normal form reaches 60 m at 100° × 60° and his far form 90 m at 30° × 30°, only scouts and snipers get a **200 m zone at 5°**, night switches the far zone off, and fog halves every distance ([mgsv-deminified-lua](https://github.com/TinManTex/mgsv-deminified-lua/blob/master/data1/Assets/tpp/level_asset/chara/enemy/Soldier2ParameterTables.lua)). The semantics are undocumented and values may vary by patch, but the pattern is clear: MGSV handles darkness and weather by shrinking range and angle, not by slowing a meter. Alien: Isolation gives every character four cones with per-kind light sensitivity, none for the creature, heavy for humans and slight for androids ([Thompson, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/revisiting-the-ai-of-alien-isolation)), and Thief's guards had five, including a motion-only cone behind them ([Leonard, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-)).

The meter's edges are where fairness is won or lost. Thief delays every rise by a filter whose length belongs to the guard's current state, which is how it implements reaction time and forgiveness, and lets awareness fall one state at a time, like a capacitor discharging ([Leonard](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-)). The Dark Mod publishes the most complete open ladder: alert thresholds of **1.5, 6, 10, 18 and 23**, ramp-down times of **5, 8, 25 and 65 s** with random spread, and grace periods of 2–3 s in which further alerts are ignored unless one beats the last rise by a set fraction or too many arrive, with its tuning history kept in the config's comments ([tdm_ai.def](https://github.com/codereader/DarkRadiant/blob/f2ac15de7a8189dfdb93747944f0105efa134fac/test/resources/tdm/def/tdm_ai.def)). The Last of Us counts a perception timer up while the player is visible and down while he isn't; a typical NPC perceives at about 1–2 s, the threshold is much higher until an NPC has seen the player once and much lower in combat, and the ray target moves from the chest in stealth to the top of the head in combat, which favours the sneaking player ([McIntosh, Game AI Pro 2](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter34_Human_Enemy_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf)). Sightline's 30% curious threshold sits near The Dark Mod's suspicious state, at 26% of its combat value.

Light is handled three ways: graded (Thief, The Dark Mod, Assassin's Creed Shadows), binary (Mark of the Ninja, Conviction), or ignored inside the front cone, as in Dishonored, whose designers rejected pools of shadow because players read a guard walking past someone in the dark as bad AI ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2012/08/21/interview-dishonoreds-harvey-smith-reboots-the-stealth-genre/)). Readability drove each choice. Thief's light gem exists because playtesters got contradictory signals about whether they were hidden ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/building-the-original-i-thief-s-i-revolutionary-stealth-system)); Conviction's black-and-white shadow filter was dropped for Blacklist after it ranked as the second most common complaint in about 80 reviews the team read, according to a press interview ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2012/07/17/directing-splinter-cell-an-interview-with-maxime-beland.aspx)); Mark of the Ninja removed an added meter that undercut its design ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-mark-of-the-ninja-i-s-five-stealth-design-rules)). Telegraphs come in four families: meters over guards' heads (Far Cry, Dishonored), HUD timers and directional warnings (Blacklist, The Last of Us Part II), cues in the world (Shadow Tactics fills the cone from the guard's eyes, with a sound sting and slow motion) and time tricks such as MGSV's optional Reflex Mode ([Wagner, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-dynamic-detection-in-i-shadow-tactics-i-); [PlayStation accessibility](https://playstation.com/en-us/games/the-last-of-us-part-ii/accessibility/)).

Walsh's chapter is candid about the price of a crisp model: a player a centimetre outside a shape is never seen, a centimetre inside is caught within seconds, and he calls the cliff unavoidable once the HUD has to match NPC state. His principle is that "player perception should win" within plausibility, and the change that made Blacklist immediately more fun was halving hearing, for certain events, in guards who were offscreen and plausibly far away ([Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)). Studios tuned all this by playtesting with debug drawing; this research found no published automated fairness test and no study of how well players predict detection. The nearest is Tremblay, Torres and Verbrugge's FDG 2014 study, in which **27 participants** judged the safer of two paths past guards in 15 levels: ten levels reached 75% agreement, and an occlusion-aware **distance-to-guard metric matched the humans on all ten**, against seven for line of sight and seven for near misses ([Tremblay et al., FDG 2014](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~jtremb59/Papers/fdg2014_paper_33.pdf)). Two of Sightline's ideas have no precedent: no shipped game documents a sky-silhouette factor, and none documents foliage that hides both ways, while Arma's AI spotting prone players through grass the player can't see through has drawn complaints for a decade, and one modder added grass checks only between player and AI because checking all pairs cost too much ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/107410/discussions/0/3192490241171731993); [Bohemia tracker](https://feedback.bistudio.com/T128621); [Bohemia forums](https://forums.bohemia.net/forums/topic/241678-acstg-ai-cannot-see-through-grass)). Scale separates Sightline too: every stealth-action vision model found stops at 60–90 m, apart from MGSV's 5° observer zone, so Sightline's 150 m standing player and 400 m marksman sit in mil-sim territory, where guard animation can no longer carry readability.

### Careful hearing measures path distance once per sound

Shipped hearing splits into two families. One gives each sound a radius in straight-line distance: Half-Life multiplies a sound's volume by the listener's sensitivity to get a hearing distance, Unreal Engine 5.8's hearing sense documents no occlusion at all, and The Last of Us and Hunt: Showdown cut the radius with raycasts ([Leonard](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-); [Epic](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/ai-perception-in-unreal-engine); [Botta, Game AI Pro 2](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter33_Infected_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf); [Crytek](https://www.huntshowdown.com/news/hunt-audio-readability-realism-and-consistency)). The other measures distance along a cheap structure so sound bends around walls, and every stealth game that cared about not hearing through walls chose it. Thief propagated sounds through its level geometry with doors muffling them, and in Deus Ex: Invisible War, whose sound programmer called its propagation very similar to Thief's, speech reaches a guard a floor down by way of the hall, the stairs and the next hall ([Leonard](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-); [GameBanshee](http://www.gamebanshee.com/ka3h4)). Blacklist abandoned sound-engine distance queries, which were sometimes extremely expensive and gave false detections whenever audio data hadn't been built, for a path over areas joined by doors and windows, and never went back ([Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)). Mark of the Ninja ran A\* over a triangle mesh of empty space, so a guard inside a sound's radius with no path to it hears nothing, and fed **the same path to the player's own audio filtering** ([Miles, Game AI Pro](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter32_How_to_Catch_a_Ninja_NPC_Awareness_in_a_2D_Stealth_Platformer.pdf)), the direct precedent for Sightline's one law of sound for guards and speakers. Hitman's rooms and portals run to about 275 rooms and 800 portals per level inside about 1 ms of main-thread time, per a search excerpt of the slides ([Boev, GDC Europe 2015](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1022774/Sound-Propagation-in)). Rainbow Six Siege takes the cheapest path by length plus accumulated turn angle plus a destruction penalty, and plays a muffled through-wall version alongside it, because occlusion alone would have worked as a wall hack ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-dynamic-audio-in-destructible-levels-in-i-rainbow-six-siege-i-)). The open-source Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup floods its grid once per turn in integer arithmetic, with walls costing 12× a cell, closed doors 8× and trees 3×, plus 25% per change of direction ([DCSS shout.cc](https://github.com/crawl/crawl/blob/master/crawl-ref/source/shout.cc)). Wave-based acoustics shipped only as offline bakes of static geometry, as Microsoft's Project Triton did for Gears of War 4 and 5, Sea of Thieves and Borderlands 3 ([Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/project-triton/)).

Guards hear logical events, not rendered audio. The Last of Us lets designers choose which sounds each character type hears and at what range, makes Infected hear about six times better than Hunters, and adds a silent logical breathing sound so a still player nearby can be sensed ([Botta](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter33_Infected_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf)). CryENGINE types its stimuli (footsteps peak at 25, weapons at 50, sight in the main cone at 100), shapes each with an attack-decay-sustain-release envelope, adds a separate bullet-rain stimulus for rounds passing close, and deliberately ignores birdsong and machinery ([Welsh, Game AI Pro](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter31_Crytek%27s_Target_Tracks_Perception_System.pdf)). Masking shipped as a visible threshold (Chaos Theory's noise meter against the ambient level), as discrete loud areas (Sniper Elite) or as ambient noise subtracted from loudness (DCSS) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell:_Chaos_Theory); [gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/sniper-elite-5/stealth/z5fe6d); [DCSS](https://github.com/crawl/crawl/blob/master/crawl-ref/source/shout.cc)), and reviewers called Sniper Elite III's masking poorly implemented and Sniper Elite 4's inconsistent ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2014-07-11-sniper-elite-3-review-war-and-pieces.html); [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/games/sniper_elite_4/b/playstation4/archive/2017/02/13/sniper-elite-4-game-informer-review.aspx)). **No game publishes a masking threshold in decibels or a localization error.** Localization in games is a set of design rules: DCSS shifts the perceived origin toward the listener and adds fuzz in proportion to how much longer the sound's path was than the straight line, Blacklist's guards look at the door into the player's area rather than at a wall, and Sniper Elite III escalates by counting shots ([DCSS](https://github.com/crawl/crawl/blob/master/crawl-ref/source/shout.cc); [Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2014-07-11-sniper-elite-3-review-war-and-pieces.html)). Psychoacoustics gives rough anchors: about 16–20 dB of masking in a vacuum-cleaner example, and localization near 1° straight ahead against about 15° to the side ([Wikipedia: Auditory masking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auditory_masking); [Wikipedia: Sound localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_localization)).

The physics of a supersonic shot is settled, and no game this research found uses it for AI. The muzzle blast lasts 3–5 ms, travels from the gun at the speed of sound and is loudest along the barrel; the bullet trails a shock cone of half-angle arcsin(1/M) whose front moves perpendicular to the cone, and Maher's model matched measured arrival times for a .308 at Mach 2.54 ([Maher 2006](https://www.montana.edu/rmaher/publications/maher_ieeedsp_0906_257-261.pdf)). Worked through for this research, the geometry means a listener near the bullet's path hears the crack arrive from a point upstream on the path, **about 55–62° off the shooter's true bearing** at Sightline's bullet speeds, while the crack-to-report gap at the target grows to about **0.47 s at 300 m** for a .308 load (computed). Suppressors cut 26–41 dB at the shooter's ear in one study and 7–32 dB in another funded by a suppressor trade association, and suppressed supersonic fire still routinely exceeds 140 dB, so the crack survives ([ENT Today on Branch 2011](https://www.enttoday.org/article/firearm-muzzle-suppressors-far-superior-to-ear-level-protection-devices-for-noise-reduction/); [Hearing Review on Le Prell 2017](https://hearingreview.com/inside-hearing/research/sound-level-suppressors-firearm-noise-reduction-implications-hearing-conservation)). Crytek's bullet-rain stimulus is the closest game analogue. For telegraphing, Mark of the Ninja's rings show the radius at which enemies hear, although they can overstate who hears because the AI uses paths, as Miles's own figure shows ([Miles](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter32_How_to_Catch_a_Ninja_NPC_Awareness_in_a_2D_Stealth_Platformer.pdf)); Blacklist's barks run from specific to generic so players learn the cause without hearing a memorable line twice; Hunt asks that a player know by ear what a sound is, where it is and what state it's in ([Crytek](https://www.huntshowdown.com/news/hunt-audio-readability-realism-and-consistency)); and The Last of Us cut the Clickers' echolocation because it couldn't be communicated, a precedent for rejecting any sense that can't be telegraphed ([Botta](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter33_Infected_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf)).

### Guard AI layers a state machine, utility and a coordinator

Shipped guard AI converges on layers. The Last of Us runs a prioritized machine of skills (investigate, flank, hide, gun combat and others), each with its own small machine, and inside each skill **17 post selectors** score candidate positions by multiplying normalized 0–1 criteria, from raycasts and paths gathered on earlier frames under budgets of up to 160 rays per frame ([McIntosh](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter34_Human_Enemy_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf)). Halo 2 chose binary relevancy over floating scores because tuning floats across twenty or more options became practically impossible ([Isla, GDC 2005](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/gdc-2005-proceeding-handling-complexity-in-the-i-halo-2-i-ai)); Guild Wars 2's Infinite Axis Utility System multiplies response-curve considerations, stops at the first zero, and uses runtime and cooldown terms to stop two decisions strobing ([Lewis, Game AI Pro 3](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro3/GameAIPro3_Chapter13_Choosing_Effective_Utility-Based_Considerations.pdf)). F.E.A.R.'s goal-oriented planner and Guerrilla's hierarchical task networks shipped and worked, but their most portable lessons are architectural: keep squad logic out of individuals, treat orders as goals a soldier weighs against fleeing danger, and choose dialogue after the decision ([Orkin 2006](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~dyer/cs540/handouts/gdc2006_orkin_jeff_fear.pdf); [Guerrilla](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/the-ai-of-horizon-zero-dawn)). Left 4 Dead attaches a reason string to every transition for live debugging ([Booth 2009](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf)), the cheapest route to explaining every reaction.

Beliefs are per-guard records filtered by perception: The Last of Us's location and timestamp, Halo 2's props, Thief's sense links. Uncertainty is a grid that spreads outward from the last known position and is cleared wherever a searcher can see; The Last of Us shipped exactly that search map, and Damián Isla built the commercial stealth game Third Eye Crime around occupancy maps ([McIntosh](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter34_Human_Enemy_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf); [Isla, AIIDE 2013](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/view/12663)). The published failure is pacing. Focus tests found The Last of Us's combat-to-search cycle averaging nearly **two minutes**, and the fix that cut it to **about 30 s** was a cheat: if the player moved more than 5 m from where the NPCs believed he was, one approached at once ([McIntosh](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter34_Human_Enemy_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf)). The newest research, Xu and Verbrugge's composite potential fields, reached a **0.80 capture rate against 0.56 for a staleness-driven state machine, 0.12 for a plain one and 0.95 for a cheating chaser**, for a single guard and without human testing ([Xu & Verbrugge, AIIDE 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.18527v1)). Generic AAA search splits hostile stimuli from distractions, checks the last known position first, then hands out hidden spots through a coordinator ([Welsh, Game AI Pro 2](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter27_Looking_for_Trouble_Making_NPCs_Search_Realistically.pdf)). Blacklist adds two cheap behaviours its developers rate highly: the first guard to witness a changed door or light claims it, and a guard who has heard a comrade for 10 s and then hears nothing for 5 s investigates, behind a long cooldown ([Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)).

Above individuals, the proven commander is declarative. Halo 3's Objectives list tasks with a priority, an activation condition and a capacity, and a greedy assignment places squads at a cost built from travel, balance and coordination; its reported downsides were designer training, squads proving the wrong unit to assign, and a bad cost function making the AI look stupid ([Isla, GDC 2008](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~rich/courses/imgd4000-d09/lectures/halo3.pdf)). Caps on convergence come from roles that must be requested and released, as in The Last of Us's Combat Coordinator, which gives a distraction sound a single Investigator, or DOOM's attack tokens ([McIntosh](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter34_Human_Enemy_AI_in_The_Last_of_Us.pdf); [Thompson](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)). Pacing directors work but read the truth: Left 4 Dead's Director builds up, sustains a peak for 3–5 s, fades and relaxes for 30–45 s from each survivor's actual intensity, and Alien: Isolation's director always knows where the player is and hints at it ([Booth](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/apps/valve/2009/ai_systems_of_l4d_mike_booth.pdf); [Thompson](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-perfect-organism-the-ai-of-alien-isolation)). Physical escalation is documented mostly by guides and players: Far Cry 3's guards run for alarm panels that can be shot out, and the alarm brings three or four riflemen in a jeep; MGSV's radio calls run long and stop if the caller drops; and Metal Gear Solid 2's headquarters sends a team when a guard misses a check-in ([Prima](https://primagames.com/eguides/far-cry-3-with-interactive-map-eguide/essentials/key-locations/outposts); [speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/users/AgentA/comments); [StrategyWiki](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_Solid_2:_Sons_of_Liberty/Gameplay)). F.E.A.R. got reinforcements for free: a squad's last survivor calls for help that doesn't exist, and players read the next enemies as the response ([Orkin](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~dyer/cs540/handouts/gdc2006_orkin_jeff_fear.pdf)). Against a player sniping from an unreachable rooftop, Blacklist runs a systemic group behaviour: a grenade under covering fire, a spoken decision to search if he slips away, retreat if he keeps killing ([Walsh](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter28_Modeling_Perception_and_Awareness_in_Tom_Clancy%27s_Splinter_Cell_Blacklist.pdf)). Killzone reasoned about suppression from the hidden threat's side, using a precomputed **64 KB line-of-fire table for 4,000 waypoints** ([Beij & Straatman 2005](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/gdce05_killzone_ai.pdf)).

Navigation needs little cleverness at Sightline's scale. Jump point search and its preprocessed form, up to 116× faster than tuned A\* in open maps, need uniform costs, which tactical costs for exposure or danger rule out; flow fields suit many guards converging on one goal; and The Last of Us's two tiers, coarse routes plus a fine local grid, match Sightline's two grid layers ([Harabor & Grastien](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~dharabor/data/papers/harabor-grastien-aaai11.pdf); [Rabin, Game AI Pro 2](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter14_JPS_Plus_An_Extreme_A_Star_Speed_Optimization_for_Static_Uniform_Cost_Grids.pdf); [Emerson, Game AI Pro](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter23_Crowd_Pathfinding_and_Steering_Using_Flow_Field_Tiles.pdf)). Floating-point divergence in lockstep games comes from transcendental functions, fused multiply-add and compiler rewrites, the hazards Sightline's math rules already exclude ([Fiedler](https://gafferongames.com/post/floating_point_determinism/)).

### One drag constant suffices inside 600 m if the instruments share it

Shipped shooters sit on four rungs. Hunt: Showdown's original weapons had no drop at all, because its designer held that a miss should be the player's fault ([Crytek](https://www.huntshowdown.com/news/inside-hunt-s-weapon-system-with-david-west)); its 2024 relaunch adds gravity only past a per-weapon drop range, according to guides; vanilla Arma 3 uses gravity plus one drag constant per round, as Battlefield from BF1 on and Escape from Tarkov appear to, according to community analyses ([ACE3 model.rs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acemod/ACE3/master/extension/src/ballistics/bullet/model.rs); [devtrackers](https://devtrackers.gg/escapefromtarkov/p/7cd2cf1d-how-to-make-snipers-snipe)); and a full point-mass solver with drag tables, atmosphere, spin drift, Coriolis and transonic jitter exists only in the opt-in, GPL-licensed ACE3 mod ([ACE3](https://ace3.acemod.org/wiki/feature/advanced-ballistics)). Bohemia itself declined to add wind to Arma 3's bullets, citing performance, time and gameplay ([Arma 3 OPREP](https://dev.arma3.com/post/oprep-marksmen-weapons)). The best-selling sniper series keeps realism optional: according to guides, Sniper Elite 4 applies wind only on its top two of four presets and moves the scope 100 m per click, and Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2 needs no correction inside about 400 m ([gamepressure SE4](https://www.gamepressure.com/sniper-elite-4/using-the-sniper-rifle/z797d3); [gamepressure SGWC2](https://www.gamepressure.com/sniper-ghost-warrior-contracts-2/shooting/z1e91b)).

The physics is easy at Sightline's ranges. A point-mass G7 solver written for this research, checked within 0.7–1.6% of Wikipedia's published .338 trajectory ([Wikipedia: External ballistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/External_ballistics)), flies Hornady's published .308 168 gr ELD Match load (823 m/s, G7 0.263) ([retailer listing](https://globalordnance.com/hornady-match-308-win-168-grain-eld-match-20-round-box-80966-upc-090255809664)) to **300 m in 0.41 s with 1.50 mil of drop and 0.40 mil of drift** in a 10 km/h crosswind, and to 500 m in 0.74 s with 3.64 and 0.73 mil (computed). All three loads tried stay above Mach 1.6 at 500 m, so nothing transonic happens in Sightline's band, and the design's half second to 400 m holds at 0.51–0.57 s. One quadratic drag constant fitted to the G7 time of flight reproduces the G7 drop within about 1 cm out to 600 m, and it has a closed form, x(t) = ln(1 + k·v₀·t)/k, that needs only `dlog`, `dexp` and division. Integration is where naive code fails: flown at one step per 30 Hz tick, semi-implicit Euler lands **18.5 cm low at 500 m**, Heun's method 0.15 cm and RK4 0.07 cm. Carrying the bullet along with the wind overstates drift six to nine times; the correct lag rule drifts by wind × (time of flight − range ÷ muzzle speed). Spin drift (about 0.1 mil at 500 m), Coriolis and muzzle-velocity spread all stay below a head's width. Open-source solvers exist under ISC, MIT, LGPL and GPL licences, but all use native transcendental math, variable steps or WebAssembly, so none can enter `src/sim` as it is ([js-ballistics](https://github.com/o-murphy/js-ballistics); [ballistics-engine](https://docs.rs/crate/ballistics-engine/latest); [py-ballisticcalc](https://github.com/o-murphy/py-ballisticcalc)).

Mil reticles make every correction decimal: a mil is 10 cm at 100 m, the usual click is 0.1 mil, and range in metres is target size in millimetres divided by mils ([Wikipedia: Milliradian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milliradian)). At 300 m the shot needs every instrument: 1.5 mil of drop and a 1.9 mil lead for a guard walking at 1.4 m/s each exceed two head widths of 0.67 mil, and the wind is about 0.6 of one (computed). Lead in mils barely changes with range, so the reticle turns it into a rule of thumb, about 2 mil for a walker, 3.5 for a jogger and 6 for a runner. Arma 3's 2014 rework is the best-documented sway model, with vertical breathing scaled by fatigue, grip, stance tiers and an oxygen-limited breath hold that spikes sway on release, but it published no numbers, and no game does ([Arma 3 OPREP](https://dev.arma3.com/post/oprep-weapon-sway-fatigue)). A guide reports that Sniper Elite III allows a breath hold only at 80 beats per minute or below ([Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/gaming/sniper-elite-3-guide)), and physiology adds little: a 1987 abstract reports champion shooters firing between heartbeats ([BJSM](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/21/1/33)), and no source measured sway by shooting position.

What makes a long shot feel earned is a visible chain of decisions followed by a delayed, legible payoff. Sniper Elite's bullet cam began as the reward for reading wind and distance, and a hidden score rations it by target movement, distance, body part and time since the last cam ([MCV/DEVELOP](https://mcvuk.com/?p=23739)). Most players stay on easy settings: 27.4% of Sniper Elite 4's Steam owners finished the campaign on Cadet and 21.5% on Marksman ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/312660/achievements)), and reviewers picked Contracts 2's long shots as its best part ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sniper_Ghost_Warrior_Contracts_2)). Physics supplies a payoff no game source mentions: at 300 m the impact's sound reaches the shooter about 1.28 s after the trigger (computed). For timing, Overwatch's high-precision input and Counter-Strike 2's sub-tick system resolve a shot at the instant between ticks when the player fired ([Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/new-feature-high-precision-mouse-input-gameplay-option/422094); [Counter-Strike Wiki](https://counterstrikewiki.com/Sub-Tick_System)), as Sightline's timed fire command already does.

### Controllers ship patches where an invariant belongs

Industry character controllers share one core: a kinematic shape moved by collide-and-slide with a bounded number of iterations (Quake III and Source both allow 4 bumps against 5 clip planes), a small offset that keeps it off surfaces, step-up and step-down passes, and a walkable slope near 45° ([Quake III source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/Quake-III-Arena/master/code/game/bg_slidemove.c); [Source SDK](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/master/src/game/shared/gamemovement.cpp); [Unreal docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/walkable-slope-in-unreal-engine)). Source's stepping tries a plain and a stepped slide and keeps whichever went farther, and Quake refuses to step onto ground too steep to stand on. Sightline's 0.45 m step equals Unreal's default and its 42° limit is stricter than all of them. The engines differ on honesty: Unity's skin width lets colliders sink that deep, and PhysX ships an overlap-recovery module that pushes characters back out of geometry ([Unity Manual](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/physics-section/physics-overview/character-control-section/class-character-controller); [PhysX docs](https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.4.1/docs/CharacterControllers.html)).

Lean's shipped failures are about where the eye is. Rainbow Six Siege moved the leaning camera from the far side of the head to its centre in 2019 to counter lean-spam, and a later patch closed a melee-plus-lean input that moved the head almost instantly ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/rainbow-six-siege/rainbow-six-siege-lean-spam)); Dark Engine speedrunners lean through doors to press buttons on the far side ([speedrun.com](https://www.speedrun.com/posts/51buw)); Dishonored abstracts the leaning body as hidden, favouring the player over honesty ([Harvey Smith's tips](https://www.witchboy.net/2012/10/)). Ground Branch's design is the most deliberate: with the weapon lowered the lean exposes just one eye, and with it raised it goes far enough for the weapon to clear the corner ([Ground Branch](https://groundbranch.com/2012/09/leaning-peeking-system)). No source gives a lean distance or a camera-roll angle. Mantles find ledges with three probes, forward at several heights for the face, downward for the top and a shape overlap at the landing pose, and The Dark Mod's first-person mantle splits the move into hang, pull, shift and push with an eased camera instead of body animation ([Amigo](https://danimtz.github.io/devpost/Mantle); [TDM 6343](https://bugs.thedarkmod.com/view.php?id=6343)); its recorded bug is that the endpoint was validated once, so anything that moved in afterward caused clipping ([TDM 4509](https://bugs.thedarkmod.com/view.php?id=4509)). Source makes a ladder its own movement type with gravity off and the ladder's normal stored, and Half-Life 2 adds dismount points that must let the player stand without intersecting geometry ([Source SDK](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/master/src/game/shared/gamemovement.cpp); [Valve Developer Community](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Info_ladder_dismount)). Prone defeats a single capsule: players report legs poking through walls and taking hits in BattleBit, Delta Force and Hell Let Loose ([Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/671860/discussions/4/3814034023686941160)), Arma's metrics call for a 50 cm gap to crawl through (search excerpt) ([Bohemia wiki](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/Arma_3:_Infantry_Metrics)), and Battlefield 2042 patched prone clipping on specific geometry ([EA](https://forums.ea.com/blog/battlefield-game-info-hub-en/battlefield-2042---update-3-2-0/7011697/replies/7011749)).

Bodies are perceivable evidence in Thief, are found by patrols rather than by an area sweep from Chaos Theory on (per a fan wiki), and fit two to a container in Hitman (per guides) ([Leonard](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-); [Splinter Cell Wiki](https://splintercell.fandom.com/wiki/Alarm); [gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/hitman-iii/bodies-how-to-get-rid-of-them/zee225)); no source gives a speed or noise value for carrying or dragging. Self-readouts moved from HUD meters (Thief's light gem, Chaos Theory's noise meter against ambient, MGS3's camo index) to the world (Mark of the Ninja's binary light, Conviction's filter, Hunt's audio) and back to small meters (Blacklist, the eye in Assassin's Creed Shadows) ([Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-mark-of-the-ninja-i-s-five-stealth-design-rules); [Ubisoft](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/assassins-creed/news/1lmnk4XTnnqRh1foViGYPR/assassins-creed-shadows-stealth-gameplay-overview)). Thief biased the player's lighting toward the floor beneath him so players could anticipate their own safety ([Leonard](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/building-an-ai-sensory-system-examining-the-design-of-i-thief-the-dark-project-i-)). The Dark Mod's light gem, rendered and read back from the GPU, shows why readouts belong in the simulation: its brightness depended on view angle and drifted after a renderer change ([TDM #50](https://bugs.thedarkmod.com/view.php?id=50); [TDM #3144](https://bugs.thedarkmod.com/view.php?id=3144)). Hunt's June 2025 update fixed silent footsteps caused by missing or wrong surface tags, proof that one mis-tagged surface silently breaks a player's ability to predict his own noise ([Crytek](https://www.huntshowdown.com/news/dev-insight-audio-design-recap-what-s-new-in-update-24)). At a fixed tick, interpolation shows the world one to two ticks late, 33–67 ms at 30 Hz, which is why Godot advises applying mouse look every frame ([Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/physics/interpolation/advanced_physics_interpolation.html)).

### Camps come from modules in a frame, tested under fixed budgets

Every shipped procedural level found places handmade pieces in a constraining frame and retries when checks fail. XCOM 2 drops parcels from banks of modular buildings into hand-crafted plots, after Firaxis judged Enemy Unknown's random maps too random (a pre-release secondary report) ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/xcom-2-procedural-level-detail/); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1025213/Plot-and-Parcel-Procedural-Level)); Spelunky lays its solution path through a 4 × 4 room grid before filling rooms from templates ([Kazemi](http://tinysubversions.com/spelunkyGen/)); Dead Cells fits hand-designed rooms to a designer's concept graph by brute force and budgets one monster per five combat tiles ([Bénard](https://deepnight.net/tutorial/the-level-design-of-dead-cells-a-hybrid-approach/)); Brogue finds chokepoints by blocking each in turn and flood-filling, places vaults behind them, and nests **fixed retry budgets of 10, 10, 50 and 1000** with rollback when an attempt aborts ([Brogue CE Architect.c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/master/src/brogue/Architect.c)). Open-world outposts in Far Cry and Ghost Recon Wildlands appear handmade, with procedural tools used only while authoring terrain, roads and settlements ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/procedural-technology-in-ghost-recon-wildlands/)). None publishes a visibility-based check of stealth difficulty, and Invisible, Inc.'s early-access players complained about its procedural levels more than anything else until Klei added ways to manipulate guards ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/82772/invisible-inc-adjusting-difficulty-for-procedural-generation)).

Research divides generators into constructive ones that must be right by construction and generate-and-test ones that keep only what passes, and insists that content players must traverse always be correct ([Togelius et al. 2011](http://julian.togelius.com/Togelius2011Searchbased.pdf)). Constraint methods are the most expressive: Smith, Butler and Popović generated puzzles that are solvable yet admit no shortcut, a condition quantified over every possible play, with answer-set programming ([FDG 2013](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/smith2013qop.pdf)), and minimal-edit repair fixes a candidate level with the fewest changes a mixed-integer program can find ([Zhang et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.06627)). Their run times depend on the solver and the machine, and Bazzaz and Cooper's 2025 expressive-range work budgets **15 minutes per attempt** ([arXiv 2504.05334](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.05334v1)); WaveFunctionCollapse is greedy and can end in contradiction ([WFC](https://github.com/mxgmn/WaveFunctionCollapse)). McGill's stealth line set the analysis method: extrude the level through time, remove each guard's field of view at each step, and search for undetected paths with rapidly exploring random trees in (x, y, t) ([Tremblay et al., FDG 2014](https://www.sable.mcgill.ca/~clump/papers/tremblay-14-measuring.pdf)). Its guard-placement study found the trees still solved levels easily with eight guards, that the slope of the distance metric was the better difficulty signal, and that generated guard behaviours were statistically indistinguishable from designers' ([Xu et al., AIIDE 2014](https://www.sable.mcgill.ca/~clump/papers/xu-14-generative.pdf)). Its test levels are all small floor plans, no code was released, and no paper found applies these methods to outdoor or long-range settings.

Several mature, deterministic tools fit Sightline better than random trees. Safe Interval Path Planning groups each cell's safe times into a few intervals and runs A\* over cell-and-interval pairs, keeping optimality and completeness when obstacle trajectories are known ([Phillips & Likhachev 2011](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/publications/sipp-safe-interval-path-planning-for-dynamic-environments)), which Sightline's pure-function routines guarantee. Radial-sweep viewsheds run in O(n log n) against O(n^1.5) for the naive method ([GRASS r.viewshed](https://grass.osgeo.org/grass84/manuals/r.viewshed.html)), and van der Sterren's terrain reasoning scores sniper spots from precomputed waypoint visibility ([Game Developer 2001](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/terrain-reasoning-for-3d-action-games)). Patrolling theory measures each cell's idleness, the time since anyone last saw it ([Machado et al. 2002](https://dblp1.uni-trier.de/rec/conf/mabs/MachadoRZD02.html)); Stackelberg security games model an attacker who watches the defender's schedule and best-responds, which is exactly a recon player ([Pita et al., AI Magazine](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/aimagazine/article/view/2173)); restricted play measures how much a game leans on one strategy by pitting agents limited to it against unrestricted ones ([Jaffe et al. 2012](https://cdn.aaai.org/ojs/12513/12513-52-16035-1-2-20201228.pdf)); and splittable, label-keyed random streams keep one feature's draws from shifting another's ([JAX](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/jep/263-prng.html)). **No published method clusters guards by mutual visibility over time**, so Sightline's sightline graph is new work.

### A perch dominates when the camp forgets, leaks, can be muted or exposes its target

No shipped game stops a perch with one lever. Sniper Elite III escalated by shot count, nearby guards turning cautious after the first, converging after the second and the whole map knowing your position after the third, and its reviewer called that last step magic and the AI the game's biggest problem ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2014-07-11-sniper-elite-3-review-war-and-pieces.html)); Sniper Elite 4 swapped the relocation meter for triangulation, with later shots letting enemies pin down the position ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2016/03/first_impressions_sniper_elite_4_on_ps4_seems_on_target_yet_again); [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/games/sniper_elite_4/b/playstation4/archive/2017/02/13/sniper-elite-4-game-informer-review.aspx)). Sniper Elite: Resistance's lead level designer says each sniping spot is visible from two or three others and none is fully covered (interview excerpt) ([Epic Games Store](https://store.epicgames.com/news/sniper-elite-resistance-interview-going-even-deeper-behind-enemy-lines)); DICE kept scope glint for Battlefield 6, dimmed after beta complaints, and press coverage argues that without it snipers on huge maps would leave their targets no fair response (excerpts) ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/battlefield-6-designer-reveals-sniper-glint-nerfs/); [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/dice-is-nerfing-sniper-glint-but-still-thinks-battlefield-6-needs-it)); MGSV's revenge system raises helmets from 20% to 70% of guards against habitual headshots, per a fan wiki ([Metal Gear Wiki](https://metalgear.fandom.com/wiki/Revenge_System_%28enemy_preparedness%29)); and Ghost Recon Breakpoint's drones exist to force players to adapt ([Ubisoft](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/354637/ghost-recon-breakpoint-why-drones-are-the-perfect-enemy)). Hitman: Sniper Assassin plays a whole mission from one nest and stays a puzzle because targets are exposed only in windows and a found body sets off an evacuation, which players trigger on purpose to bring targets into the open ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2018/06/08/hitman-sniper-assassin-is-a-fun-but-brief-prologue-to-hitman-2/); [gameplay.tips](https://gameplay.tips/guides/4176-hitman-2.html)). Crytek's stance on camping in Hunt matches the owner's almost exactly, a valid strategy but never an auto-win, and its main answer was compound layouts with more defensive cover rather than harsher AI ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/cryteks-hunt-showdown-gets-its-first-big-content-update/)).

Critics and players describe single-position clears as trivial under four conditions: the camp forgets (Sniper Elite's guards back on patrol beside bodies), the player gets information he couldn't have (MGSV's companion Quiet clearing outposts alone; Wildlands' sync shot killing up to four at once, through walls according to players), escalation can be cut from range (shooting Far Cry and Wildlands alarm panels), or the objective is always exposed ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2014-07-11-sniper-elite-3-review-war-and-pieces.html); [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/gross-15-reasons-quiet-ruins-metal-gear-solid-v-the-phantom-pain/); [GameSpew](https://www.gamespew.com/2017/03/six-tips-getting-started-ghost-recon-wildlands/); [Prima](https://primagames.com/eguides/tom-clancys-ghost-recon-wildlands-eguide/welcome-to-bolivia/special-missions/el-muro)). Even the best AI counter-snipers disappoint: a reviewer says Sniper Elite 5's AI can't sustain the pressure a human invader brings (excerpt) ([GameByte](https://gamebyte.com/reviews/sniper-elite-5)). Two numbers carry over to Sightline (computed). A ±5° bearing error at 300 m puts the believed perch about 26 m to the side, close to Sniper Elite III's 30 m relocation, so honest beliefs alone would create relocation pressure without a meter. And with a 1.5 s reaction window and about 0.4 s of flight to 300 m, a trio in mutual view is beyond one bolt-action perch, while a pair depends on Lab B's bolt and re-aim time, which makes clusters the structural counter to the perch.

## Everywhere prior art cheated, Sightline can demand a proof

The compromises above fall into five kinds, and each collides with a rule Sightline already enforces. Knowledge leaks across the perception boundary (The Last of Us's 5 m search trigger and instant broadcast of sightings, directors that read the player's true state) collide with rule 13. Asymmetries between what the player sees and what the guard sees (Arma's grass, Siege's eye beside the head, Dishonored's hidden lean, Mark of the Ninja's overstated rings) collide with rule 14 and the one law of sound. Machine-dependent budgets (milliseconds per frame, solver time-outs, a light gem read back from the GPU) collide with rules 2 and 18. Invisible discontinuities (Blacklist's centimetre cliff, Sniper Elite's inconsistent masking) collide with the fairness rule that every detection is telegraphed. And tuning by feel is the norm everywhere: Ghost Recon Breakpoint retuned detection after launch, adding 0.2–1 s and stretching the delay between sighting and radio call from 8 to 12 s, and The Dark Mod keeps its tuning history in config comments ([Breakpoint patch notes](https://devtrackers.gg/ghost-recon-wildlands/p/b7104cde-tom-clancy-s-ghost-recon-breakpoint-patch-notes-title-update-3-0-3); [tdm_ai.def](https://github.com/codereader/DarkRadiant/blob/f2ac15de7a8189dfdb93747944f0105efa134fac/test/resources/tdm/def/tdm_ai.def)). Sightline's own build carries two of these asymmetries today, a canopy that hides a guard from the player but not the player from the guard and a bush that hides the player without blocking his view out, and its renderer draws no shade although shade multiplies detection by 0.6, which is Thief's problem from before the light gem ([Phase 1 handoff](../phase-1-handoff.md); [Phase 1 plan](../phase-1-plan.md)). None of these checks is expensive, because the hard parts of the prior art are cheap at Sightline's scale. A naive bucket-queue flood benchmarked in Node 22 during this research covered a 21 m footstep on the 0.5 m site grid in about 0.2 ms and the whole 150 m site in about 3.4 ms, against a 33 ms tick, and because every guard reads his own cell from the source's flood, the cost doesn't grow with the number of listeners (an in-house measurement, to repeat in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit under A11). The ballistic closed form costs a handful of operations per query, and safe-interval search and the perch checks are bounded searches over a few hundred thousand cells. The one cost to plan for is Lab D's analysis: rasterizing 16 guards' views at 600 samples of a five-minute cycle approaches 10⁸ cell visits by this research's estimate. The constraint Phase 1 actually faces is perceptual, not computational: whether a player can read light, skyline, periphery and masking at 150–400 m in a wireframe world.

## Each lab starts from the best and measures past it

### Lab A: turn every factor into a boundary the player can see

Lab A should start from Blacklist's structure: rays to head, chest and feet with a minimum per stance before the meter moves, nested zones in which the most inclusive sets the range, and time that grows linearly with distance inside a zone. On top go Thief's and The Dark Mod's asymmetric ladder (a rise delayed by the current state's dwell, a fall that steps down one state at a time to a raised floor, grace periods, and spread drawn from the guard's own stream) and Dishonored's split, in which darkness matters at range and facing matters up close ([Harvey Smith's tips](https://www.witchboy.net/2012/10/)), which suits a sniper's distances. Hearing should start as Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup's design on Sightline's 0.5 m site grid: one bounded flood per sound event from its source, in integer steps with a bucket queue and a fixed neighbour order, which every listener and the player's speakers read at their own cell. Add Siege's second, through-wall route (a grid raycast counting walls at a decibel loss each, with the louder route winning), Crytek's typed stimuli, The Last of Us's hearing multipliers by character kind and awareness state, and Thief's rule that a sound arrives from the doorway it came through, recovered by walking the flood's parent cells back to the last cell the guard can see.

Prior art stops helping at Sightline's numbers. The sight table's product runs from about 0.003 (peripheral, prone, still, dark, a third exposed) to 2.4 (core, standing, sprinting, lit, skylined), an **800× spread**, and with rate scaling alone any exposure eventually completes unless decay outruns fill. Making 50 m (prone, still, in cover) and 150 m (standing and walking in the open) both edges where fill equals decay needs the distance term to fall about 25× between them, roughly the cube of distance, where inverse square gives only 9×. MGSV and Shadow Tactics solve the same problem with range caps per stance and light, and MGSV, Alien and Blacklist all keep the wide periphery short, whereas Sightline's 0.3 peripheral factor applies at full range. Hiding the first 30% of the meter makes Blacklist's edge cliff even less visible. Several shipped fixes break Sightline's rules: Blacklist's offscreen halving reads the player's camera and breaks the one law of sound; The Dark Mod's habit of thinking less often at distance stays deterministic only if each slice is a pure function of tick and guard id and the skipped time accumulates, or the behaviour fingerprint moves; Unreal's radius hearing and The Last of Us's raycasts can't route sound around buildings; Triton's bakes need static geometry, while Sightline generates its camps per seed; and The Dark Mod's light gem depends on the GPU. Nothing shipped models foliage both ways, a skyline, or the crack's false bearing.

The lab can pass every model above in four measurable ways. First, rebuild the meter so each factor is a boundary the honest view can show: stance and light as range caps in the MGSV and Shadow Tactics style, periphery as a short-range zone, Blacklist's linear time inside a zone, and a ramp of rate across a few metres at each edge in place of a cliff. That changes how the design doc's sight table is read, so it is the owner's call, but the doc's own ranges need something like it. Prove it with the scenario runner's first table (A3), swept over distance, stance, movement, light, cover, zone and skyline, asserting the design's ranges, monotonicity (more exposure never detects more slowly) and a minimum number of ticks from the first visible cue to 100% for every combination outside an explicit, drawn contact zone. Second, make sight and sound symmetric by construction and test both bit for bit: foliage as per-volume transmittance multiplied along each body ray in both directions and drawn from the same volumes, which removes today's canopy and bush asymmetries; and the player's mix computed from the same flood and the same masked threshold as a guard standing at the player's cell. Masking can be a threshold shift in Sightline's audibility units, with ambient levels summed as power and the threshold set a tunable 15–20 dB below the masker, as the psychoacoustics suggests; under the 6 dB-per-halving law a door's loss of L dB multiplies effective distance by 10^(L/20), so a 10 dB door counts as 3.2 times the distance. The debug view should draw the true contour where clarity reaches 1, never a circle. Third, model the shot's sounds physically: the report from the muzzle, the crack from the upstream point Maher's geometry predicts, the impact from where it lands, each scheduled at its exact arrival (sound covers 11.4 m per tick, so a gunshot's wavefront is itself the schedule). A scenario can then show that guards near the path of a suppressed shot search the bullet's side of the valley, offset by the predicted angle, which no shipped game does. Fourth, measure predictability rather than only the pass mark. Next to the 80% gate, trial mode (A12) should report what a distance-only predictor scores on the same trials, since Tremblay found distance is what people judge by, and break borderline accuracy down by the factor that flips each outcome. Testers clearly beating the distance-only score is the evidence that light, skyline, cover and periphery are readable, and any factor whose borderline trials sit near chance needs a cue or a simpler rule before the second sitting. Fuzz runs that log every detection with its factor breakdown close the loop on the rule that every detection can be explained.

### Lab B: one ballistic function for the bullet and every instrument

Lab B should start from realistic-lite as Sniper Elite's top presets and Contracts 2's reticle marks define it, made physically consistent: gravity and one drag constant per load in the style of vanilla Arma, the lag rule for wind, a mil reticle with a zero dial in 100 m steps and mil holds between them (a target 50 m from the nearest zero step sits 11–34 cm off between 250 and 550 m, computed, so both tools are needed), Arma's sway model, Sniper Elite's rationed payoff, and the timed fire command the foundation pass already built, which carries the lag and view angles of the frame the player saw.

No shipped single-player game documents its solver; the full-simulation reference is a GPL mod; every open-source solver needs porting to `detmath` and a fixed step (and ACE3's transonic jitter would have to draw from the bullet's own stream); and no game publishes sway amplitudes or frequencies. Insurgency: Sandstorm's bullet leaves the muzzle rather than the screen centre, which conflicts with Sightline's rule that a shot leaves from where the camera looks ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2021/09/13/creating-immersion-and-intensity-in-insurgency-sandstorm/)). The extras of full simulation add under 0.2 mil at 100–500 m, opacity for no perceptible gain. The trap specific to a 30 Hz simulation is integration: a bullet flown by semi-implicit Euler at one step per tick lands 18.5 cm low at 500 m, nearly a head, so a reticle drawn from an exact formula would lie by that much.

The lab can go past every shipped model in four measurable ways. First, one ballistic function drives everything: the bullet, the reticle's hash marks, the rangefinder's drop readout, the wind readout and every assist. It can be the one-constant closed form, exact at any sub-tick time with no integration state beyond fire time, origin, direction, drag constant, muzzle speed and wind, or Heun's method once per tick. A sweep over loads, 100–500 m, winds and ±30° slopes should assert that the drawn hold and the simulated impact agree within 1 cm; worked in the bore's frame, the closed form stays within 0.05 cm across the line of aim on such slopes (computed). A community test of Tarkov found field of view distorting the reticle so badly that a 100 m zero behaved like 200–300 m ([devtrackers](https://devtrackers.gg/escapefromtarkov/p/7cd2cf1d-how-to-make-snipers-snipe)); this test rules that class of bug out. Second, make earned measurable as decision sensitivity. At 300 m a head is 0.67 mil wide, so a centred shot misses once its error passes 0.33 mil, and a CI test can assert that every skipped decision crosses that line (drop 1.5 mil, lead 1.9 mil for a walker, 0.4 mil of wind at 10 km/h, about 0.35 mil for a 25 m ranging error, computed) while dispersion at the earned stance stays well inside it. A proposed starting ladder, unsourced and meant for tuning, puts free-standing sway well above a head, prone unrested at 0.5–0.8 mil, prone rested at 0.25 and prone rested on a held breath at 0.1 or less, so a 300 m headshot needs prone and a held breath and a torso shot doesn't. The telemetry (B10) then decomposes every miss into range, lead, wind and sway error in mils, so the owner's verdict on the 300 m shot can be checked against what actually decided each one. Third, make the payoff physically true: the thwack scheduled at flight time plus return time (about 1.28 s at 300 m), dust on a miss so the player can correct like a spotter, a trace drawn from the simulated path, and optionally a kill cam that is only a slowed replay of the recording, rationed by a Sniper Elite-style score. Fourth, evaluate sway as a pure function of state and time (incommensurate sines, a heartbeat pulse whose phase advances at rate × dt, hashed grip noise, and the breath hold as a state machine with Arma's release spike), so the shot leaves exactly where the drawn reticle pointed at the click's sub-tick instant, tested across frame rates as the fire command was. Swept hits between two ticks' poses carry an error of about the path's 1.4 mm bend per tick (computed), so no sub-stepping is needed. Taste call 3 then becomes a choice of assist layers over one physics, which the reception data supports, since most players never leave the easy settings.

### Lab C: no motor mode gets an exception

Lab C starts from Quake's and Source's bounded collide-and-slide (fixed bump and plane counts, try both and keep the farther step, check a step's landing slope as well as its height), an offset kept outside surfaces in the style of Rapier, Jolt and PhysX rather than Unity's penetrating skin ([Rapier](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/character_controller); [Jolt](https://jrouwe.github.io/JoltPhysics/class_character_virtual_settings.html)), Siege's head-centred lean camera and Ground Branch's distinction between a peek and a weapon lean, The Dark Mod's phased mantle with an eased pull, Source's ladder mode and Half-Life 2's validated dismount poses, Thief's floor-biased lighting, and Godot's advice to apply mouse look every frame while the simulation interpolates.

Each shipped controller accepts something Sightline's motor rule forbids: penetration within a skin, overlap recovery that pushes bodies out (which would hide bugs that ought to trip a fault), clipping fixed map by map, a mantle endpoint checked once. Bohemia's tracker reportedly judged a full fix for prone wall clipping to be beyond Arma 3's animation system (search excerpt) ([Bohemia tracker](https://feedback.bistudio.com/T77845)). Rapier and Jolt can't enter `src/sim`, because their WebAssembly internals fall outside the exactly specified math the simulation allows. No source gives lean distance, roll angle, mantle height limits, prone turn rate, or carrying and dragging speeds and noise, so all of them are the lab's to set. Dishonored's hidden lean and Tarkov's automatic vault both work against prediction: one hides real exposure, the other makes noise the player didn't choose ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/escape-from-tarkov-is-finally-adding-vaulting-and-players-are-elated)).

The step past prior art is that no motor mode gets an exception, and the fuzz run proves it. Lean, mantle, ladder, prone and carry each move along a path whose every pose is checked every tick, and rate limits keep the universal rise rule intact: a 1.2 m mantle over 0.6 s rises 0.067 m per tick, and any mantle or climb slower than 13.5 m/s never exceeds the 0.45 m step. Prone becomes a capsule or chain of circles along the facing (C2), grown over the transition so it never ends a tick inside anything, turning by the largest yaw that keeps every part clear (found with a fixed number of bisections), pivoting near the chest so the view stays steady, and pitched from the terrain under head and feet; a carried or dropped body reuses it. The measure is **zero invariant violations over the weekly 40-hour fuzz run** once its bots lean into walls, mantle into moving guards and turn prone between walls, the cases Battlefield, BattleBit and The Dark Mod patched after shipping. Lean puts the eye at the centre of the simulated head that guards target, so a property test can show that whenever the player can see a guard past a corner, the guard has a line to the player's eye. Self-readouts stay in the world, as C7 intends: shade drawn where `lightAt` finds it, footsteps and drag scrape at their simulated loudness and calibrated like the footstep voices, and an audit that every terrain cell's surface tag matches its rendered material, the bug Hunt shipped. The noise and visibility trials (C8) should report accuracy by the factor that flips each outcome (surface, gait, distance, masking, light), and add a small cue on the body, the low-clutter form Double Agent and Shadows used ([SuperCheats](https://www.supercheats.com/xbox360/tom-clancys-splinter-cell-double-agent/3176/stealth-tips/); [Ubisoft](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/assassins-creed/news/1lmnk4XTnnqRh1foViGYPR/assassins-creed-shadows-stealth-gameplay-overview)), only for the two rules the world can't show: ambient masking and light from behind the player. Because every tuning rate is per second, a blind comparison of 30 Hz and 60 Hz in the owner's sitting is possible in principle, and it would be the first published comparison of tick rates for character feel.

### Lab D: certify the camp before anyone plays it

Lab D starts from XCOM 2's plots and parcels, with the compound's perimeter, gate and roads as the plot and the towers, buildings, generator and motor pool as parcels drawn from labelled forks. It adds Spelunky's rule of laying the solution first, here a perch line, a low-exposure corridor, a reachable sabotage node and two exfil corridors placed before any filler, so validation mostly asks whether the fill broke the skeleton; staffing as points spent on roles under the 16-guard cap, after Dead Cells and RimWorld ([RimWorld Wiki](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Raid_points)); Brogue's chokepoint map, which puts sentries at real chokes and checks that exfil routes share none; Xu's skeleton roadmaps with a grammar of stops and scans for patrols that stay pure functions of time; McGill's space-time extrusion of guard vision with Tremblay's distance metric as the exposure cost; and feature-first terrain, with two or three ridge spines placed on chosen bearings 150–500 m out before noise and erosion and then verified by viewshed, so overwatch comes from a few sectors and not a ring (D3).

Research stops short of Sightline's problem in four places. Its stealth analysis was built for small floor plans around random-tree search, which is incomplete and answers differently per stream, so it can estimate variety but can't certify that an infiltration path exists. Its strongest guarantees come from constraint solvers whose run time depends on the machine, which rule 18 forbids at runtime, and its neural generators would compute differently between browsers. Nobody has published a way to cluster guards by mutual visibility over time, or a share of guards or target exposure that one vantage point may see. And the plan's starting rule for D8 hides an ambiguity the sniping research flags: counting a guard as seen from a perch if he is ever in view over the cycle is a union, which makes the no-single-perch rule hard to pass and the long-range approach rule (two perches that see half the guards) easy to pass, unless both use one time-averaged share or a minimum visible duration. The analysis has a real cost too: at the estimated 10⁸ cell visits for full rasterization it must be budgeted by iterations from the start, with coarser sampling outside the hard core or evaluation only at candidate cells, and every map and set must be iterated in sorted order so the outputs hash the same in every engine.

Lab D can exceed both the shipped and the academic state in five measurable ways. First, validate infiltration exactly with Safe Interval Path Planning over the routines' known trajectories on the 0.5 m grid, allowing exposure that stays below Lab A's fill rates rather than banning all exposure, and show completeness by agreeing with brute-force space-time search on small camps; keep random trees only to estimate how many distinct routes exist. Second, build the sightline graph as directed witness edges, each carrying the intervals of the cycle when it holds, with strongly connected components as clusters, and certify every cluster by a bounded search for a kill order inside the reaction window or a split by sabotage or distraction, modelled as Borodovski's action-at-a-time branches ([Borodovski & Verbrugge 2016](https://www.sable.mcgill.ca/~clump/papers/borodovski-16-analyzing.pdf)); then hold each camp's graph to the design's shape of a few isolated entries, two or three small clusters and a hard core, and check D's own sight model against A's detection at sampled points, as D7 plans. Third, make no-single-perch an exhaustive check over the finite perch list, Smith's quantification over play made finite, with one pinned counting method and three companion rules the prior art motivates: each perch overlooked by a marksman post or a reachable counter-perch (Sniper Elite: Resistance), no alarm box or antenna within fire of a perch that sees many guards (Far Cry, Wildlands), and an escape route interceptable only from perches other than the best overview (Hitman's evacuation exploit). Fourth, prove plan diversity before the reviewer looks: run restricted-play bots (long range only, infiltration only, systems only) on each camp, cluster their best plans by the reviewer's own signature fields, as Campbell, Tremblay and Verbrugge clustered player paths ([Tremblay publications](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~jtremb59/Publications.html)), and plot expressive range across 100 seeds (cluster count against hard-core size, best-perch dominance against infiltration exposure), so the reviewer's ten distinct signatures confirm what the bots predicted rather than discover it. Fifth, keep repairs local and seeds stable: minimal-edit repair as bounded greedy search over a fixed list of operators (move a sentry along its arc, shift a patrol's phase, swap a module, add cover, re-time a light circuit), each attempt on its own labelled fork, with dressing hashed from seed, label and cell so a repair touches only its own cells ([Eiserloh, GDC 2017](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024365/Math-for-Game-Programmers-Noise)). Recording the iterations each seed used lets the 90-of-100 result also show how close every seed came to its budget.

### Lab E: a camp as competent as a cheater, without cheating

Lab E starts from The Last of Us's template, a prioritized machine of awareness states with multiplied 0–1 scores inside each and a coordinator that issues roles, plus Thief's asymmetric transitions, Halo 2's short-lived stimulus behaviours, Guild Wars 2's response curves and cooldowns, Zoo Tycoon 2's rank-then-weight choice with weighted draws from the guard's own stream ([Dill, Game AI Pro 2](https://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter03_Dual-Utility_Reasoning.pdf)), and Left 4 Dead's reason code on every transition. The commander should be Halo 3's declarative Objectives over guards rather than squads, with Killzone 2's objective weights ([Straatman & Champandard 2009](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/GAIC09_Killzone2Bots_StraatmanChampandard.pdf)) and ties broken by task index, then guard id, and orders that arrive as percepts the guard weighs, as in F.E.A.R. Beliefs about the player live in one camp-level search map, The Last of Us's diffusing grid or Isla's occupancy map, fed only by reports. Blacklist's claiming, missing-comrade and unreachable-rooftop behaviours, Metal Gear's check-ins and interruptible calls as scheduled events, role tokens to cap convergence, and Left 4 Dead's pacing shape complete the kit.

Nearly every one of these leans on the truth somewhere. The Last of Us broadcasts sightings to every NPC at once and fixed its two-minute search cycle with a 5 m check on the real player, both directors read true state, and Sniper Elite III's reveal ignores who heard what. Their budgets are milliseconds per frame, which have to become iteration counts with cursors kept in state. Per-guard occupancy maps over a 400 m valley at 2.5 m would put about **410,000 numbers** into a state tree that is hashed every tick, so the map belongs to the camp, or to a bounded window around the incident. Off-the-shelf libraries don't fit as they stand: Yuka's memory records live in class instances and hold values that don't survive JSON, and behaviour-tree libraries keep state outside a plain-data tree ([Yuka](https://github.com/Mugen87/yuka)), while a second collision authority such as ORCA would break the single-motor rule. The published counter-sniper record is weak, and no shipped game publishes radio delays, check-in timings or triangulation numbers.

The goal worth setting is a camp as competent as a cheater without cheating, and it can be measured. Implement Xu and Verbrugge's baselines inside Sightline (a random walk, a plain state machine, a staleness search and an omniscient chaser) and report the honest brain's search efficiency, such as time to re-find an evasion bot and coverage of the believed region, as a fraction of the omniscient one's on the same camps; then soften the camp deliberately through the pacing layer's caps, so it is fair by design rather than weak by accident, the trap critics found in Sniper Elite. End searches from belief, never truth: trigger the check-approach and the search on time since the last percept and the spread of the search map, and hold the cycle near 30–45 s in scenarios, The Last of Us's post-fix pace without its cheat, while the no-omniscience test covers the whole brain. Make triangulation honest: every guard who heard a report or a crack turns it into a bearing with error from his own stream that grows off-axis and with excess path, reports reach the commander by voice or delayed radio, and the commander fuses them in a fixed order into an ellipse that shrinks with repeated shots. Two numbers then test it. Across seeded runs, the camp's one-sigma ellipse should contain the true perch about 39% of the time and its two-sigma ellipse about 86%, the two-dimensional Gaussian rates, which shows the camp's uncertainty is calibrated; and the relocation distance a shooter needs should emerge from it, about 26 m at ±5° and 300 m, against Sniper Elite III's scripted 30 m. Counter-snipers hunt rather than scan: they move to overlooking posts, sweep candidate perches in belief order with ties broken by perch id, and glint along the sun vector and the scope axis, Battlefield's long-proven telegraph, with Blacklist's unreachable-rooftop response where they can't reach. The perfect shooter on the best perch (E12) must fail on every validated camp, and the converse matters as much: a scripted shooter who relocates, splits clusters and times shots must still succeed, or the camp is unwinnable rather than fair. Escalation stays physical and interruptible: the alarm run is a motor journey, a radio report is an action with a duration whose scheduled delivery is cancelled if the speaker drops, a check-in is two scheduled events, and Lockdown is set only by an alarm box's or radio's change of state. Adaptation within a mission comes only from incidents the player caused and could see, following Alien's rule of never unlocking behaviour from player deaths: a habituation ledger by stimulus and cell, posture floors on decay, and incident memory such as a perch with casualties, each reason-coded so the owner's count of unexplained reactions can reach zero.

## A dozen numbers nobody has published

Several quantities the labs need have no published value in any game or paper this research reached, so each lab has to measure or decide them and record where its number came from. For Lab A these are a masking threshold in decibels, localization error by angle and clarity, and how badly listeners misjudge distance; the human auditory-distance literature, such as Zahorik's reviews, wasn't reached and should be read before range estimates are tuned. For Lab B they are sway amplitudes and frequencies by stance, breath-hold length beyond guide claims, and how a crack's loudness falls with miss distance. For Lab C they are lean distance and roll, mantle height limits, prone turning rate, and the speed and noise of carrying and dragging. For Lab E they are radio delays, check-in intervals, how many shots Sniper Elite 4 or 5 needs before a fix and how wide its error is, how fast a sweep should close, and how long a search should last beyond Sniper Elite 5's few minutes. For Lab D they are the share of guards and of target exposure one vantage point may see, perch spacing and relocation path lengths, and whether a 25% cap on target exposure feels fair rather than frustrating. Beyond numbers, no study measures how accurately players predict detection or their own exposure, with or without a meter, and none compares 30 Hz and 60 Hz for character feel. Several primary sources weren't read in full (Isla's occupancy-map paper, Klei's and Firaxis's GDC talks, Audiokinetic's spatial-audio documentation); the search budget ran out before Escape from Tarkov, Metal Gear Solid V's internals, The Last of Us Part II, Hitman's World of Assassination and several procedural stealth games (Heat Signature, State of Decay, RimWorld's base generator); and much of the Metal Gear, Far Cry and Ghost Recon escalation material rests on guides and fan wikis. Those belong in the labs as hypotheses to test, not as specifications.

## Conclusion

The ceiling of prior art in each lane is not an algorithm Sightline lacks; it is a guarantee nobody could give. Studios tuned fairness by watching players because their simulations couldn't be swept, replayed bit for bit or quantified over, and their cheats (a search trigger on the true position, deafness offscreen, a map-wide reveal) were the price of that blindness. Sightline can make each of the design doc's promises to the player a test that runs on every push, and that, more than any single mechanic, is where it can stand above the games it borrows from. Two consequences follow for the order of work: Lab A should restructure its factor table before trial mode starts, and Lab D should pin how perch coverage is counted before coding D8. Both are cheap to settle on paper now and expensive to discover in a failed exit test: the first decides whether the owner's blind trials measure readable cues or unreachable arithmetic, and the second whether validation's two perch rules can pass on the same camp.

On the owner's concern that one overlook clears a camp, the evidence points to a conjunction rather than a lever: generator geometry that keeps something decisive out of every perch's view part of the time (counter-perches, an indoor target, alarms out of reach, an escape seen from elsewhere), plus a camp whose beliefs converge honestly and whose escalation can be interrupted. Sightline can prove that conjunction from both sides on every validated seed, a perfect shooter on the best perch failing and a relocating planner succeeding, which no sniper game has shown. The richest by-product of honesty is the crack. Modelling it physically gives the suppressor its real meaning and gives guards a mistake the player can learn to cause, sweeping the bullet's side of the valley instead of the shooter's, a mechanic that no shipped game has and that comes from physics rather than script.
